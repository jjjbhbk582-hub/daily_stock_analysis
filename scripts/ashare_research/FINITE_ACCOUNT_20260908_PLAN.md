# Finite-account consolidated experiment — fixed before performance inspection

Date of research report: 2026-09-08; frozen price cutoff remains 2026-09-04. All periods have already been observed by this project. No pristine out-of-sample claim, no automatic parameter search, no brokerage, no orders, no main-branch changes.

## Signal definitions
All indicator windows are computed on valid quoted bars; at least 250 earlier valid bars are required. No use of future pivots. Four complete policies, not a claim that every difference is caused by a single factor:
- RSI6: previous RSI6<20, current RSI6 and close both increase. Technical exit RSI6>=60 or close below the previous ten closes' minimum. Shared maximum holding period ten market sessions and 8% close-trigger stop apply in this new finite-account model; it is not numerically identical to the prior independent-sleeve reference.
- RSI2_PULLBACK: RSI2<10, close>MA200 and MA200>its level twenty quoted bars earlier. Technical exit close>=MA5. Maximum holding period ten market sessions.
- BAND_RECOVERY: yesterday close below yesterday MA20 minus two population standard deviations, today close above today's lower band and above yesterday's close. Technical exit close>=MA20. Maximum holding period ten market sessions.
- CONFIRMED_TRENDLINE: a local high is recognized only three quoted bars after its date, when it is the unique maximum of a seven-bar window. Use the most recent two confirmed highs, separated by 10–90 quoted bars, with the later high lower. Extend their line forward. Enter only if the same pair was already known yesterday, current close crosses above that line, close>MA60 and MA60>its level five bars earlier. Technical exit close<MA20. Maximum holding period forty market sessions. No backdated buy point, no BACKSET/ZIG future function.
RSI recursion uses zero seeds and gives 50 if its denominator is zero.

## Scope and deliberately conservative provisional admission
Mainboard codes only; ST and unknown states are rejected at both signal and execution. After any historically observed ST event, do not readmit that stock without verified restoration evidence. This permanent historical quarantine is a narrower research universe than the user's ordinary non-ST universe. It uses only information up to the current date, never removes pre-risk trades, and does NOT replace full dated listing-phase evidence. Known termination decision cases remain blocked. Missing complete metadata is still a certification blocker, even if numerical performance is positive.

## One finite account
Initial budget 100,000 RMB, at most five simultaneous positions, no leverage, no cash interest. New position value capped at 10% of signal-day equity, not continuously rebalanced. Price floor 3 RMB. Previous twenty quoted days' mean traded amount at least 50 million RMB. Rank simultaneous signals by that mean amount, with code as deterministic tie-break. Entry shares are 100-share lots, at most 0.1% of signal-day volume.
At completed signal close, fix raw-price limit close*1.03 rounded down to a cent, quantity and cash reserve. Attempt once next market session. Missing or too-high opens cancel; no same-open replacement, no anticipated sale proceeds or freed holding slots. Double-delay scenario attempts after two sessions.
Hypothetical fees: commission 0.03%, minimum 5 RMB each side; transfer 0.002% before 2022-04-29 and 0.001% thereafter; sell stamp input 0.1% before 2023-08-28 and 0.05% thereafter. Earlier tax histories are not fully reconstructed. Slippage 0.1% each side with adverse cent rounding. Double-cost doubles all hypothetical fee and slippage inputs, including the minimum.
Close-triggered 8% economic-return stop and technical/time exits submit next-session sale requests. Pending sales persist through missing or nontrading days. Approximate negative gap blocks at -9.8% for non-ST and -4.8% for ST; positive +9.8% gap blocks new buys. These are explicit diagnostic execution proxies, NOT certified legal limits or auction matching. New ST while held forces exit requests without erasing losses. Stale terminal holdings are separately marked and fully written down in an extreme residual-value check.

## Price and corporate-action caveat
Raw entry prices are recovered as adjusted price/factor and rounded to cents. Raw-volume recovery follows the source normalization. Entry quantities and commissions use the raw-price budget. Thereafter, constant adjusted economic units proxy corporate-action value. Equivalent exit shares can differ from true cash/bonus-share settlement. Every trade whose factor changes is counted, and the model must remain real_share_ledger_verified=false. Do not call this a fully reconstructed real-money account.

## Fixed scenario matrix
Four separate cash-start periods: 2001-2008, 2009-2014, 2015-2020, 2021-2026-09-04. Four policies per period. Thirteen scenarios per policy: liquidity-ranked base, double costs, one extra execution day, and ten fixed random-priority seeds 0–9 under base costs. Total 208 finite-account scenarios. Save every scenario's trades, orders, daily equity and cash events, plus source coverage and synthetic-account limitations. Ten random priorities are sensitivity controls, not an optimization menu or independent historical samples.
No winner is declared using an isolated high-return row. Reporting includes cumulative and annualized returns, peak-to-trough drawdown, terminal residual exposure, win/loss scale, individual-trade adverse excursion, fee impact, priority sensitivity and both gains and losses. A history-positive result is at most a research candidate; incomplete lifecycle, real corporate-action accounting and native Tonghuashun checks still block trading certification.
